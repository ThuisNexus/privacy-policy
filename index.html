Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our multiplayer drawing game ("the App") collects, uses, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Player Name: Used for identification within the game.
  • Chat Messages: Messages sent during gameplay.
  • Gameplay Data: Drawing actions, scores, and game interactions.

2. How We Use Information

The collected data is used solely to:

  • Enable real-time multiplayer gameplay
  • Display player identities and scores
  • Synchronize game state between players

3. Data Storage and Security

We do not store personal data permanently. Data is processed temporarily during gameplay sessions. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data during transmission.

4. Personal Data

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to operate the App:

  • Render.com – for hosting backend servers and enabling multiplayer connectivity.

6. Children's Privacy

This App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention

Gameplay data is not stored permanently and is only retained for the duration of an active game session.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. User Rights

Since we do not store personal data, there is no persistent user data to access, modify, or delete.
